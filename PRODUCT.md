# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and collaborators reviewing Azwar Fadzlin's engineering projects and experience.

## Product Purpose

Present projects, qualifications and current FIUU Product Development internship experience, with a downloadable resume and a reliable way to make contact.

## Brand Personality

Technical, professional and approachable. The redesign expresses this through a light Apple-inspired desktop, iOS-like app icons, and clear engineering evidence.

## Anti-references

Reference: https://elenagonci.com/ and the owner's supplied desktop and full-page screenshots. Borrow the spacious composition, rounded widgets, blue folders, dock, and window presentation while using Azwar's own identity and content.

Avoid dark neon backgrounds, invented project screenshots, decorative controls with no behavior, and a desktop layout merely scaled down on mobile.

## Design Principles

- The owner has authorized a complete redesign and selected the existing Next.js/React stack. The old dark-theme preservation requirement applied only to earlier repairs.
- Follow REDESIGN-PLAN.md for staged work and CONTENT-MAP.md for content and asset decisions. DESIGN.md currently records the old design baseline.
- Use hybrid navigation: dock links scroll to sections; project folders open responsive detail windows.
- Include a Malaysia clock, calendar, and personal photo. Rearrange content for phone and tablet layouts.
- Use colorful rounded app icons and blue project folders against quiet light surfaces.
- Use the current resume and owner-provided information for factual updates.
- Make visible controls perform the action their labels describe.
- Keep contact, project links and resume access easy to find.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, narrow screens, associated form labels and reduced-motion preferences as part of the requested issue fixes.
